The listening protocol

Judge only the selected lens using the original 30-second excerpts. A grade of 2 or 3 means relevant. Adjust your device to a comfortable volume and keep it fixed. Replay as much as you need; the three-second gate only prevents accidental ratings.

Each batch pools and deduplicates the top five text, Adam sparse and full-space neighbours, plus one randomly sampled comparison. Ordering is shuffled; methods, ranks, captions and group labels stay hidden. A late repeated pair measures your consistency. A random comparison is never assumed to be irrelevant.

Reference tracks are reproducibly sampled across the lens’s metadata/caption groups, including the unspecified pool. Missing or ambiguous evidence is a valid reason to choose unsure. These are single-listener research judgments over stratified references, not population-wide listening scores.

Saved annotations live in your private persistent storage. Changing a rating preserves the earlier event in the audit export. Scores compare the same fully judged result pools; unfinished pools never count as failures.
